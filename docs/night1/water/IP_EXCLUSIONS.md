# Water: IP exclusions

> Homage fan game. Not an official Marvel, Sony or Insomniac game; no affiliation.

The water piece adds no copied names, logos, art or textures. Every texture is generated by `unreal/WebHomage/Scripts/build_water.py` from seeded noise (`T_WaterNoise`, `T_WaterRipple`)
or from the city layout geometry (`T_WaterShore`, `T_WaterContact`). The Gerstner wave constants come from this repository's own browser port (`src/world/waves.js`).
The reference stills / clip of the real game (`~/spiderman-learnings/refs`) were only viewed and measured, never copied into the repository. No `ts_ads`, `ts_signs`, `city_signart` or vehicle-livery cell is used.
